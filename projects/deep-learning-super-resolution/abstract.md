# Abstract

This research presents a comprehensive study on high-efficiency modulation methods for Fourier single-pixel imaging (FSPI) based on deep learning algorithms, addressing the critical challenges of low imaging frame rates and poor image quality in traditional single-pixel imaging systems. The study develops three innovative approaches to enhance FSPI performance: (1) An online binarization modulation method that converts grayscale Fourier basis patterns to binary patterns, significantly improving DMD refresh rates while maintaining reconstruction quality through generative adversarial networks (F2SPI-GAN). This method achieves rapid high-quality target image reconstruction even at low sampling rates (1-3%), with experimental validation showing superior performance over traditional methods. (2) A sampling strategy optimization network (S2O-FSPI) that automatically learns and optimizes sampling strategies through end-to-end training, incorporating spatial attention mechanisms to focus sampling on information-rich frequency regions. The network simultaneously outputs optimal spatial frequency sampling strategies and high-quality reconstruction results without ringing artifacts. (3) A geometric topology analysis of optimal sampling strategies across different target scene categories, revealing that similar data categories exhibit similar geometric topologies in their optimal sampling strategies, with dominant sample categories determining the geometric topology in mixed datasets. The research leverages extensive datasets including automotive images, natural scenes, medical images, and facial datasets, achieving significant improvements in peak signal-to-noise ratio (PSNR) and structural similarity index (SSIM) compared to conventional methods. Experimental results demonstrate that the proposed approaches can achieve high-quality image reconstruction at sampling rates as low as 1-3%, effectively suppressing ringing effects and improving overall imaging efficiency. This work provides a robust framework for advancing FSPI technology in applications requiring high-speed, high-quality imaging such as remote sensing, laser radar, and real-time optical imaging systems.
