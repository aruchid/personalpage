# Abstract
 
This study presents a comprehensive performance enhancement analysis of the Prioritized Experience Replay Multi-Agent Deep Deterministic Policy Gradient (PER-MADDPG) algorithm, demonstrating significant improvements over the original MADDPG approach in multi-agent reinforcement learning scenarios. The proposed algorithm incorporates four key enhancements: (1) prioritized experience replay mechanism (PER) for efficient sample utilization, (2) independent experience pools for each agent to enhance learning autonomy, (3) TD-error-based priority updates for optimal experience selection, and (4) importance sampling weights to maintain learning stability. Performance analysis reveals substantial improvements across multiple metrics: convergence speed is accelerated by 50% (reaching optimal performance in 500 steps versus 1000 steps for original MADDPG), profit levels show remarkable increases ranging from 25% for 300 MW units (480→600 yuan) to 100% for 600 MW units (1500→3000 yuan) and 88% for 1000 MW units (1700→3200 yuan), while maintaining high trading volume stability across different capacity units (1000 MWH for large units, 500-600 MWH for medium units, and 150-300 MWH for small units). Market efficiency metrics demonstrate 40% reduction in profit volatility, more balanced profit distribution, and improved trading volume allocation. The enhanced algorithm achieves a final profit of 7000 yuan (2.9% increase over original MADDPG's 6800 yuan) with significantly improved convergence characteristics and market stability, making it particularly suitable for complex multi-agent systems requiring efficient resource allocation and stable market operations.
