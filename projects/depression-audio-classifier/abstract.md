# Abstract

This project presents a depression detection system based on multimodal deep learning that integrates speech and text analysis for intelligent mental health assessment. The system leverages state-of-the-art natural language processing and audio analysis technologies, specifically employing BERT for semantic understanding and Wav2Vec2 for acoustic feature extraction, combined with ensemble learning algorithms to fuse multimodal features for enhanced detection accuracy. By analyzing both vocal characteristics and linguistic expressions, the system can objectively and accurately assess individuals' mental health status, providing a scientific auxiliary tool for depression diagnosis that significantly improves diagnostic accuracy and efficiency compared to traditional clinical methods. The proposed solution addresses the challenges of inconsistent diagnostic criteria, early identification difficulties, and low assessment efficiency in conventional depression diagnosis, offering potential applications in mental health screening, clinical auxiliary diagnosis, research and development, and remote medical support, thereby contributing to the standardization and accessibility of mental health services.
